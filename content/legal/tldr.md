# Summary of Privacy and Terms

This document summarizes our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. It is provided for convenience and does not modify or replace the full documents. In the event of any conflict, the full documents control.

## About Tren

Tren is a desktop utility client compatible with Minecraft and Minecraft-adjacent clients, together with an account portal at portal.tren.gg. Access is provided on a recurring subscription basis. Tren is not affiliated with Mojang Studios or Microsoft Corporation.

## Information Collected

Tren collects the following:

- Your email address and a cryptographic hash of your password.
- A one-way hash of hardware identifiers from each authorized computer, used to enforce per-seat licensing. The hash cannot be reversed to identify the underlying device.
- Configuration data that you elect to synchronize to the cloud.
- Operational and audit records, including authentication events, downloads, and IP addresses.
- A Stripe customer reference and basic subscription metadata. Stripe processes payment information directly; Tren does not receive or store card numbers.

Tren does not sell personal information and does not engage in advertising.

## Service Providers

The Service relies on a limited set of third-party providers, each engaged for a specific operational function: Stripe (payments), Render (application hosting and database), Vercel (web frontend), Cloudflare (DNS, edge caching, object storage, and download authorization), and Sentry (error reporting).

## Subscriptions and Billing

Subscriptions are recurring and renew automatically until canceled through the Portal. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the then-current billing period.

Pricing, plans, and tiers may change at any time. Existing subscriptions are not affected during their current billing period. Notice will be provided in advance of any change affecting renewal.

Accounts are issued to individual users. Account sharing, lending, and transfer are prohibited. Licenses may not be resold absent express written authorization from Tren.

## Subscription Expiry and Termination

Upon expiry, cancellation, or non-payment, access to the Client is disabled immediately. If the account has not been terminated by Tren, Portal access is retained for purposes of managing the subscription, updating settings, and deleting the account, and user data is retained until the account is deleted.

Termination by Tren may result in revocation of all access, forfeiture of unused subscription time, and ineligibility for refund.

## Account Deletion

Account deletion is processed immediately and is permanent. Active Stripe subscriptions are canceled, authentication sessions are revoked, and the account record is hard-deleted. Related records are removed by cascade. Audit and entitlement records are retained but disassociated from the deleted account. Residual copies may persist in encrypted backups until those backups expire on their normal rotation.

## Security

Internal access to production data is restricted on a need-to-know basis. No system is fully secure; users will be notified of any breach as required by applicable law.
